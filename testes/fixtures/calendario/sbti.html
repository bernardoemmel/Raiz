The SBTi is opening a tender process to identify a Senior Technical Expert to provide specialist input to support the delivery of its Forests, Land, and Agriculture (FLAG) guidance revision.

The tender is open to consultancies, freelance contracts or secondments from a partner organization. For more information on the scope of work and how to apply, read the full tender document. SBTi is accepting applications until 30 November 2026.

The SBTi has revised the draft Automotive Net-Zero Standard and reclassified it as a Sector Specification, an extension of the Corporate Net-Zero Standard V2.0.

Previous drafts have already been through two rounds of public consultation. Because of this change in format, the SBTi is opening an additional public comment period from 13 November to 6 December 2026 before publishing the final version.
